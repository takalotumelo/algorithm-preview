    The problem

    Most marketing starts with the answer. Then goes looking for the question.

    Ask an SEO agency what your business needs and it starts with keywords. A paid media agency starts with media. A social agency starts with platforms. A GEO agency starts with AI visibility. Each looks at your business through the service it sells.

    That is how businesses end up with content nobody asked for, campaigns aimed at the wrong buyer, and AI visibility work chasing questions their customers never ask.

    Algorithm starts somewhere else. Before we decide what to build, fix or spend money on, we find out how your market actually works. Not as you assume it works. Not as one SEO tool describes it. As it is.

    Pathfinder establishes market reality before we try to change what the algorithms see.

    What is Algorithm Pathfinder?

    A map of your market, built from evidence.

    Before anyone builds a road, someone surveys the land. Pathfinder is that survey for your business.

    It reads your website, studies your competitors, measures what people search for in each of your markets and gathers what real customers say in forums and reviews. It checks how AI assistants describe your category and who they recommend. Then it brings everything together in one interactive dashboard your team can explore through a share link, with no login.

    An audit asks "What is wrong with what already exists?" Pathfinder asks "What is happening in this market, what matters most and where is the opportunity?"

    Pathfinder in one paragraph. Algorithm Pathfinder is a market intelligence engine built in South Africa by Algorithm. It combines customer research, search demand, competitor analysis, voice-of-customer research and AI search analysis into one evidence-backed dashboard. It is how every Algorithm Organic engagement begins, and it shapes the SEO and GEO work that follows.

    What Pathfinder looks at

    Six questions every strategy should answer first.

    None of these is new on its own. The value is in putting them side by side, so a customer complaint, a search pattern and a competitor gap can be read as one story.

    Customer intelligence

    Who is the valuable customer?

    Who buys, what drives them, what worries them and how they make the decision.

    Demand intelligence

    What is the market looking for?

    What people search for, how often, and how the language changes between markets and stages of the decision.

    Competitive intelligence

    Who owns the attention today?

    Where competitors are strong, where they are exposed and where you are genuinely different.

    Voice of customer

    What are people actually saying?

    The questions, praise, complaints and doubts customers post in forums and reviews, in their own words.

    Market intelligence

    What is changing in the category?

    Where the growth is, what is shifting and which opportunities are worth real money.

    Discovery intelligence

    Where do people find their answers?

    How Google, AI assistants, social, video and communities each shape the decision.

    Pathfinder and GEO

    Before AI can recommend you, you need to know what it already believes.

    Generative Engine Optimisation is the work of getting your brand named and cited inside AI answers. Most GEO starts by checking whether your brand shows up. Pathfinder starts a step earlier, by working out what the AI answers in your category are built from.

    It looks at how AI assistants describe your market, which competitors they name, which sources they lean on, and where your brand is missing or misunderstood. That tells us whether each gap is about information, authority, or how machines connect your brand to what you sell. Three different fixes, and guessing wrong wastes a quarter.

    The engines disagree. Across 170,720 citations in South African AI answers, 87.8% of cited domains appeared on only one engine, according to our research on who AI engines cite in South Africa.

    GEO is not the future of Organic. Entity-led Organic is, and GEO is one of its most important new expressions. Every Pathfinder dashboard closes on an AI and LLM strategy.

    • Which competitors they name, and how often
    • Which sources shape their answers
    • Where your brand is missing or misunderstood

    How it works

    Four steps from question to evidence.

    1. 01

      Set the brief

      We confirm your website, the two or three competitors that matter most, up to four target markets and anything we should look at closely.

    2. 02

      Research the market

      Pathfinder crawls your site and your competitors', measures search demand in each market and researches the live web for customer questions, quotes, concerns and trust signals. Everything is logged with its source.

    3. 03

      Build the picture

      AI writes up the findings from that evidence only. Every number carries its source, estimates are labelled, and thin data is shown as thin. Percentages and totals are calculated, never written by the model.

    4. 04

      Decide what to do

      You get the interactive dashboard. Our strategists use it to decide which customers, products and services to prioritise and what to build first.

    Pathfinder provides the evidence. Algorithm provides the judgement. AI gives the research its breadth and speed. People stay accountable for what matters and what happens next.

    Recommendations you can trace back to a reason.

    Pathfinder builds an evidence chain behind every recommendation, so when you ask "why?", there is an answer you can check.

    Frequently asked questions

    Pathfinder, in plain terms.

    What is Algorithm Pathfinder?+

    A market intelligence engine built in South Africa by Algorithm. It researches your customers, competitors, search demand, what customers say in forums and reviews, and how AI assistants describe your category, then brings it together in one interactive, source-attributed dashboard.

    Is Pathfinder an SEO audit?+

    No. An audit asks what is wrong with what already exists. Pathfinder asks what is happening in your market, what matters most and where the opportunity is. The technical audit of your site happens separately.

    How does Pathfinder help with GEO?+

    It studies how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude describe your market, which competitors they name, which sources they rely on, and where your brand is missing or misunderstood, so the GEO work fixes the right problem.

    Where does the data come from?+

    Your website, your competitors' websites, search demand data for each target market, and live research across the forums, review sites and communities where your buyers talk. Every finding is tied to its source.

    How do you stop the AI from making things up?+

    The AI writes only from the evidence Pathfinder has gathered. Every number carries a source, estimates are labelled, and thin data is shown as thin. Percentages, counts and totals are calculated in code, not written by the model. Then Algorithm's strategists review it and make the decisions.

    What does my business receive?+

    An interactive dashboard about your own market, shared through a link with no login, covering the nine sections above.

    When is Pathfinder delivered?+

    In the first month of an Algorithm Organic engagement, at every budget, as part of the Strategy Sprint. See how Algorithm rolls out Organic.

    What is the difference between Pathfinder and Entity Pathfinder?+

    Pathfinder works at market level and decides where to focus. Entity Pathfinder works at the level of one prioritised product, service or category, and works out what pages and information need to exist for it.

    Does Pathfinder work for any type of business?+

    Yes. It was first built for travel and hospitality and has since been rebuilt to adapt to any business, whether you sell products, services or to other businesses.
