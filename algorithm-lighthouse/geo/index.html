    The shift

    Your competitors are being recommended. Are you?

    People are skipping Google. They're opening ChatGPT and asking "What's the best insurance provider in South Africa?" They're asking Perplexity to compare accounting software. They're asking Gemini to recommend a marketing agency. And they're trusting the answers.

    This isn't a future trend. It's happening now, at scale. Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) have emerged as the most important new disciplines in digital marketing because the underlying behaviour has already changed.

    Unlike traditional SEO, where you can see your rankings, most organisations have no visibility at all into what AI is saying about them.

    If you can't see what AI is saying about your brand, you can't control it.

    What is Lighthouse GEO?

    Purpose-built intelligence for AI search.

    Lighthouse GEO is a proprietary AI visibility monitoring platform built in South Africa, for a market where nothing else existed. It monitors what the major AI engines are saying about your brand, your competitors and your category.

    We built it using direct API integrations with each AI platform: not scraping, not proxying, not guessing. Real queries, real responses, real data. Scored, structured and turned into strategy you can act on.

    Where global AEO/GEO tools exist, they were built for mature markets with single-language audiences and established digital infrastructure. South Africa has neither. That forced us to build something better.

    From SEO specialists to GEO market leaders

    We built it because nothing else existed.

    Algorithm started as a search agency. SEO was our foundation, and it still is. But as AI engines began reshaping how people discover brands, we saw the gap before most of the market knew it existed.

    No tool could tell us what ChatGPT was saying about our clients. No platform could score AI visibility. So we built one from scratch, using proprietary API-based monitoring rather than scraping or screenshot analysis.

    The result is Lighthouse GEO: built in South Africa, battle-tested in one of the world's most complex digital environments, and ready for any market on the planet.

    What CMOs are asking

    Straight answers.

    What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?+

    GEO is the practice of optimising your brand's visibility in AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. Where SEO focuses on ranking in Google's link-based results, GEO focuses on being cited and recommended in AI-generated answers. AI engines prioritise entity authority, content structure and citation sources rather than traditional ranking signals.

    How does Lighthouse GEO actually monitor AI engines?+

    Direct API integrations with each AI platform. Not scraping, not screenshots, not third-party proxies. We query the same APIs that power the AI responses your customers see.

    How quickly can we see results from a GEO strategy?+

    Most brands see measurable score movement within 30–60 days of implementing the quick wins. Structural gains compound over 90 days and beyond, and AI engines update citation patterns more frequently than Google rankings change.

    Is this only relevant for South African brands?+

    No. The platform monitors AI visibility in any language, market and category. Clients in the UK, US, EMEA and across Africa use the same platform.

    Do we need to change our existing SEO strategy?+

    No. GEO and SEO are complementary. Strong SEO feeds AI visibility, because AI engines often cite high-authority web content. Lighthouse GEO adds a new dimension to your strategy.

    What does the Lighthouse GEO analysis cost?+

    Pricing is scoped to the complexity of your competitive landscape: number of entities, languages, markets and competitors. It's available as a once-off report, as part of a retainer, or as a periodic refresh.
