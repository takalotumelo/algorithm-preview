    The problem

    Search has fractured. And it's fractured differently in every market.

    Your customers aren't just searching on Google anymore. They're asking ChatGPT in Afrikaans. They're searching TikTok in isiZulu. They're watching YouTube tutorials in Sesotho. They're reading Thai forums, German specialist communities and Spanish subreddits, and making purchasing decisions based on what they find.

    Traditional competitive intelligence tools only see a fraction of this landscape. They track Google rankings in English. Maybe a few paid channels. They miss the conversations happening on social platforms, AI engines, and in languages that don't fit neatly into a Western martech stack.

    The result is a blind spot that grows wider every quarter. In multilingual, multi-platform markets like South Africa, this isn't an edge case. It's the default reality.

    The real question isn't whether your brand shows up on Google. It's whether you can even hear the conversation.

    What is Algorithm Lighthouse?

    One analysis. Six dimensions. Total clarity.

    Algorithm Lighthouse is a proprietary competitive intelligence platform built in South Africa, one of the world's most complex digital markets. Eleven official languages. Dozens of active search and social platforms. An audience that moves fluidly between Google, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, AI chatbots and community forums, often in the same session.

    We built Lighthouse because no existing tool could see what we needed to see. Not just rankings. Not just one platform. The full picture, across every search surface, in any language, in any market. And then we turn that picture into strategy.

    The six pillars

    Six dimensions of competitive intelligence.

    Each pillar covers a distinct dimension of your brand's digital visibility. Together they give you the complete picture no single tool or audit can provide.

    01

    GEO Visibility Report

    Where does your brand appear when AI answers the question?

    AI-powered search engines are reshaping how people discover brands. Lighthouse maps your visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and other generative AI platforms, scoring how often your brand is cited, by which models, and for which queries.

    AI visibility scoringShare of voice across LLMsCitation source analysisGap identification vs competitorsPrompt-level brand tracking

    Real exampleA financial services client discovered they were completely absent from ChatGPT responses for their core product category, while their main competitor was cited in 73% of relevant prompts. Lighthouse identified the content gaps driving the disparity.

    02

    Market Share Analysis

    Who owns the conversation on social platforms?

    TikTok, YouTube, Reddit and community forums are where your audience researches, compares and decides. Lighthouse analyses your brand's share of conversation across every relevant platform, in the languages your audience actually uses.

    Social platform mappingVernacular search trackingCompetitor content analysisShare of conversation scoringCultural context reportingPlatform-specific opportunities

    Real exampleFor a South African retailer, Lighthouse revealed that 40% of product research in their category was happening on TikTok in isiZulu, a surface and language their marketing team had never monitored. Competitors were already active there.
