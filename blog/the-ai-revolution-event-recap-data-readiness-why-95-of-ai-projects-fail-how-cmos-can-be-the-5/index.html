    The AI Revolution Event Recap: Data Readiness: Why 95% of AI Projects Fail | How CMOs Can Be the 5%

    13 Nov 2025Graeme Stiles4 min read

    By – Jamie-Leigh Barnett

    Algorithm recently hosted “The AI Revolution: What it means for you as CMOs,” where we cut through the hype to provide a clear AI Roadmap for achieving Real Impact. The consensus was clear: AI won’t replace CMOs, but CMOs who use AI will replace those who don’t.

    The most critical insight for marketing leaders? Success isn’t about buying more tools; it’s about fixing the messy data foundation first

    The CMO Challenge: Readiness Over Hype

    Graeme Stiles, Algorithm’s CEO & Founder, framed the conversation by noting the promise of AI, delivering breakthroughs in Profitability, Personalisation, Productivity, and Precision. However, this potential is only realised if it’s built on the right foundation. The stark reality is that 95% of AI projects fail to add business value.

    For CMOs feeling “Over-whelmed, Over-Measured, Under-Informed,” the only way forward is Clarity. The battle is no longer about tools, as everyone has access to the same technology; it’s about readiness and the ability to “see the signals others miss”

    Ready to transition from the 95% to the 5%? Book your complimentary AI Data Readiness Review now!

    Data Is the Fuel: Eliminating the 4 Faces of Data Chaos

    Derik Nieman, Director of Data, laid out the foundational truth: Data is the Fuel that powers the AI engine. The reason most projects fail comes down to organisational data chaos, which he distilled into The Four Faces of Data Chaos:

    • Frankenstein Tech Stack: Too many unconnected tools that create confusion instead of clarity, causing teams to waste time reconciling numbers.
    • Vanity Metric Focus: Celebrating clicks and impressions while ignoring true business metrics like revenue, margin, and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), leading to wrong decisions.
    • Silos and Politics: Teams working in isolation, creating blind spots and leading to “turf wars over who gets credit” while competitors win.
    • No Source of Truth: Conflicting reports across teams, eroding executive trust and causing Marketing to lose influence in the boardroom.

    The 4-Step Journey to Data Clarity

    Algorithm’s solution to this chaos is a structured approach that establishes a Single Source of Truth:

    • Tracking Foundations: Audit and standardise all tags, pixels, and UTMs (GTM, GA4, Meta) to ensure clean conversion data. If the inputs are wrong, every number downstream is wrong.
    • What to Measure: Shift the focus from collapsing third-party data to prioritising First-Party Data and implementing a structured data layer to make that data actionable across every channel.
    • Data Warehouse (The Truth): Implement a unified data warehouse (like BigQuery, Snowflake, or Redshift) for structured events. This breaks down silos and transforms data into one clean pipeline, establishing one truth, one version of ROI for the whole business.
    • Visual Layer: Create dashboards that pull exclusively from the warehouse (not individual platforms). This ensures that Finance and Marketing align on definitions and KPIs, which ultimately leads to faster, bolder decisions and accelerated growth.

    Stop the Data Chaos. Start driving ROI. Let us help you fix your data layer.

    From Readiness to Real Impact: Predictive Power

    Once the data foundation is solid, AI can move marketing from a reactive state to a proactive one.

    • Marketing Mix Modelling (MMM): MMM utilises AI to measure the true contribution of various inputs (ad spend, seasonality, promotions) against business performance. This leads to AI Insights, such as how an Optimised Budget Allocation can result in a 23% increase in conversions as we saw with one of our clients
    • Predictive Modelling: This allows you to forecast critical outcomes like Customer Lifetime Prediction, Churn Prediction, and Creative Fatigue Detection.
    • The Next Frontier: The roadmap leads to Agentic AI Systems running on MCP Servers. These are systems that move from being a passive tool to an Active Agent, capable of retrieving, interpreting, and even initiating actions based on live data, answering complex queries in real-time.

    The Human Edge: Why CMOs Remain the Strategic Core

    The panel discussion, moderated by Kelvin Jonck, shifted the focus to the Human Edge in an AI World. The consensus among panelists, Graeme Stiles, Sarah Lake, and Google’s Artwell Nwaila, is that human ingenuity is the ultimate differentiator.

    • Measuring What’s Valuable: The discussion centered on moving away from “valuing what’s measurable” (like the usual sentiment and engagement metrics) to “measuring what’s truly valuable”. As AI does the analysis of the data, the human role pivots to asking the bigger, unexpected strategic questions that deliver “gold nuggets” outside the obvious numbers.
    • The New Scarcity: Algorithm CEO Graeme Stiles noted that a data-first agency’s new scarcity is the human ambition that pushes clients toward bold, first-mover projects rather than simply defaulting to the machine’s super-efficient path.
    • Moulding Future Talent: Sarah Lake addressed the challenge of automation handling junior tasks, stressing the agency’s role in quickly moulding new professionals to find new ways to add value and learn their craft.

    Google Showcase: The Accelerant for Strategy

    The Google session, led by Artwell Nwaila and Nana Gyima,, reinforced that AI Innovation is accelerating and “Marketers don’t need an AI strategy; they need AI to power and amplify their existing business strategies”.

    • Real ROI: Early AI Adopters are seeing an average 1.6x increase in total revenue.
    • AI Maturity: Google’s framework moves businesses through four stages: Essentials, Scaling, Leading, and Transforming.
    • Key Tools for CMOs: Google is rolling out tools like Topic Mine (to predict market trends for an estimated +240% revenue impact), YouTube Personas (for targeting, seeing a +179% increase in ROAS), and Generative AI for creative through platforms like Veo.
