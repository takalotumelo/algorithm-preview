    Unlock Your YouTube Performance: Introducing Algorithm VPS : The Agentic Video Performance System

    18 Nov 2025Graeme Stiles2 min read

    By – Graeme Stiles

    At Algorithm, we see a problem in the digital economy: Brands are investing more than ever in video, yet most YouTube channels remain unoptimised, underperforming, and disconnected from measurable results. As Graeme Stiles, our CEO, put it: “Brands have invested in video – but most of that content is invisible”.

    Video is the definitive language of search, discovery, and decision-making. However, few brands treat YouTube like the performance channel it truly is. CMOs recognise video’s importance, but the crucial piece missing is the system that connects creativity to tangible commercial results.

    That is why, following our recent “The AI Revolution: What it means for CMOs” event, we are thrilled to announce the launch of Algorithm VPS (Video Performance System).

    Algorithm VPS changes the narrative, transforming your static video libraries into structured, data-driven ecosystems designed to deliver tangible performance and growth. It’s the intelligent, scalable, and data-grounded system you’ve been missing.

    The Engine Under the Hood: Agentic AI and Our 4-Layer Framework

    VPS isn’t just another tool; it’s a continuous, learning system. It combines data intelligence, performance marketing logic, and agentic AI automation into one powerful platform that constantly improves over time.

    The core of its success lies in our proprietary four-layer optimisation framework. This framework rigorously analyses:

    • Audience intent
    • Keyword trends
    • Market behaviour

    …to precisely engineer every video for maximum discoverability, engagement, and conversion.

    “VPS brings that system to life, intelligent, scalable, and grounded in data.”

    – Graeme Stiles, CEO of Algorithm

    Future-Proofing Your Brand Authority in the Age of AI Search (AEO)

    The world of search is evolving rapidly. Algorithm VPS is designed not only for today’s performance landscape but for the next evolution of discoverability: AI Search and Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO).

    As large language models (LLMs) increasingly reference YouTube videos within conversational search results, optimised video content is becoming a crucial signal of brand authority.

    As Simon Lloyd, our Global Director of Growth & Retention, notes: “AI doesn’t just read websites – it references videos. VPS ensures your brand’s content is structured to be found, cited, and surfaced by AI systems, giving you visibility in the next generation of search”.

    Why Algorithm VPS is the Smart Investment

    By merging automation with expert human strategic oversight, Algorithm VPS guarantees your AI recommendations are aligned with your overall business objectives. The benefits go directly to your bottom line:

    • Faster Optimisation Cycles: Move beyond slow, manual processes.
    • Consistent Output Quality: Maintain excellence at scale.
    • Lower Costs: Reduce the expense associated with traditional manual optimisation.
    • Measurable, Repeatable Results: Turn video from a passive cost center into an active, compounding growth driver.

    With our tiered packages and pricing models, enterprise-grade YouTube optimisation is now accessible to businesses of all sizes.
