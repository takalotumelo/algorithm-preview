    A Guide to SEO Product Descriptions and Google Shopping

    27 Jun 2023Graeme Stiles6 min read

    Supercharge Your Online Business: A Guide to SEO Product Descriptions and Google Shopping

    As an online business owner, the digital world can feel like an alien planet, especially when you’re new to the realm of search engine optimisation (SEO) and e-commerce platforms.

    To succeed online, you need a solid understanding of SEO-optimised product descriptions and how platforms like Google Shopping can increase your online visibility. Let’s explore how these two digital marketing elements can work hand in hand to boost your e-commerce business.

    What is SEO and Why it Matters for Product Descriptions

    When it comes to e-commerce, SEO plays a pivotal role in product descriptions. These descriptions not only inform potential customers about your product’s features and benefits, but they also contribute to your website’s SEO performance.

    How? When your product descriptions contain the keywords that potential customers use in their searches, search engines are more likely to rank your pages higher in the SERPs, making your products more visible to the customers who are looking for them. But, SEO in product descriptions isn’t just about keyword insertion.

    It also involves crafting unique, compelling descriptions that appeal to customers and set your products apart from competitors.

    Investing time and resources into creating SEO-optimised product descriptions can significantly enhance your online visibility, drive more traffic to your site, and ultimately, boost your sales.

    Why do unique product descriptions matter?

    A common pitfall is the duplication of content. Duplicate content product descriptions can harm your SEO rankings because search engines might deem your website as unoriginal or of low quality. Unique product descriptions, however, tell search engines that your content is valuable and exclusive, giving your site a boost.

    To write SEO-optimised product descriptions, consider these steps:

    • Keyword Research for Product Descriptions: Begin by identifying the words or phrases your potential customers are using to find products like yours.
    • Meta Descriptions for E-commerce: Make sure your meta descriptions include your target keywords and summarise your product accurately.
    • Conversion-oriented Product Descriptions: Your descriptions should not only be SEO-friendly but also enticing to customers. Include all necessary product information, highlight key features, and use persuasive language.

    What is Google Shopping?

    Google Shopping is an online platform where customers can search for, compare, and shop for products. As an e-commerce business, leveraging Google Shopping can significantly boost your online visibility and sales.

    How to Increase Visibility on Google Shopping

    Leveraging SEO strategies can help improve your product visibility on Google Shopping, a leading platform for e-commerce businesses. Here are some Google Shopping best practices:

    • Optimise Your Product Titles:

    Your product titles are the first thing customers see on Google Shopping, so make sure they are clear, descriptive, and keyword-rich. Use high-volume keywords at the beginning of your titles, as they’re crucial for Google’s algorithms and shoppers alike.

    • Use High-Quality Images:

    Images significantly impact your click-through rates. Ensure you use high-resolution images that accurately represent your products. Well-lit, clear images can lead to higher customer engagement and can differentiate your products from competitors.

    • Complete and Detailed Product Descriptions:

    Google uses the information in your product descriptions to determine relevance for user searches. Make your product descriptions unique, compelling, and full of relevant keywords without overstuffing them. Provide as much detail as possible, including the product’s features, benefits, and specifications.

    • Optimise Your Product Types:

    Categorising your products accurately can significantly improve their visibility. Google Shopping uses product types to show your products for relevant category searches. Make sure your product types are accurate and as detailed as possible.

    • Use Relevant Product Attributes:

    Attributes like color, size, gender, material, and others can help your product appear in more specific, relevant searches. These attributes provide more information to Google about your product, improving its ability to match your product with a shopper’s search.

    • Encourage and Manage Product Reviews:

    Customer reviews can affect your Google Shopping performance. Positive reviews can improve your product’s visibility and enhance consumer trust. Encourage customers to leave reviews, respond to feedback, and manage negative reviews appropriately.

    • Implement Product Schema Markup:

    Schema markup is a type of microdata that helps search engines better understand your content. By implementing product schema markup, you can provide Google with more detailed product information, improving your chances of ranking higher in Google Shopping.

    • Regularly Update Your Product Data:

    Google Shopping favors up-to-date product data. Ensure you regularly update your data in Google Merchant Center, particularly for aspects like price changes, delivery options, product availability, or changes in the product description or image.

    What information is displayed in a Google Shopping SERP?

    A Google Shopping SERP usually shows the product image, title, price, store name, and product rating.

    Google Shopping SERPs can be more effective than traditional text-based Google listings for a few reasons. First, humans are naturally visual creatures. We process images faster than text. This means visual content can immediately grab our attention and be understood quicker than text.

    Additionally, visual content, such as an image of face cream, for example, can convey in-depth information and evoke emotions more effectively than text alone. If you’re looking for a specific face cream, a high-quality image can immediately show you the packaging, the texture of the cream, and even its application, which would be challenging to fully grasp from a text description alone.

    This visual clarity enhances the likelihood of a user clicking on your product listing, as it sets clear expectations of what they will find on your page. As such, visual SERP results can significantly improve user experience, engagement, and conversion rates for face cream listings.

    How SEO Product Descriptions and Google Shopping Work Together

    In the digital marketing ecosystem, SEO-optimised product descriptions and Google Shopping are two sides of the same coin, functioning synergistically to elevate your online presence and sales.

    This synergy starts with understanding that Google Shopping uses your product data from the Google Merchant Center and the information available on your product website page, which includes your product descriptions. Therefore, the better optimised your product descriptions are for SEO, the better they will perform not just on your own website, but also on Google Shopping.

    Optimised product descriptions rich in relevant keywords make it easier for Google to understand what your products are about, which aids in serving them to users who are searching for those keywords.

    This is particularly crucial for Google Shopping, where the competition for visibility is intense. A well-optimised product description can help your product stand out, drawing more traffic and conversions.

    The use of unique product descriptions is favored by Google’s algorithm, helping to avoid issues of duplicate content that can adversely affect your rankings. Essentially, SEO practices implemented in your product descriptions have a ripple effect, enhancing your performance on Google Shopping, and driving more visibility and sales for your e-commerce business.

    Embracing SEO strategies for online business owners doesn’t have to be daunting. By crafting unique product descriptions and leveraging Google Shopping best practices, you can enhance your online presence and attract more customers.
