    The performance marketing problem

    Most agencies optimise channels. Not the system connecting them.

    You've hired agencies before. One for SEO. Another for paid media. Maybe a third for analytics. Each optimises their silo, reports their metrics, and declares success. But revenue doesn't move.

    The problem isn't your channels. It's that nobody owns the system that connects them. When SEO doesn't inform paid, when data doesn't shape creative, when conversion strategy lives in a different spreadsheet from your media plan, you get activity without growth.

    A performance marketing agency should deliver a system, not a collection of services. That's what Algorithm builds.

    Why Algorithm is different

    A different kind of performance marketing agency.

    We don't sell channels in isolation. Every engagement is an integrated performance marketing system where SEO, paid media, CRO, data and AI reinforce each other.

    Revenue, not metrics

    We measure success by commercial outcomes. Pipeline growth, customer acquisition cost, revenue per channel. Not impressions, not clicks, not vanity dashboards.

    Architecture, not optimisation

    Before touching a single campaign, we diagnose the full demand-to-revenue journey. Then we design the architecture. Then we execute. Most agencies skip straight to step three.

    Frequently asked questions

    Performance marketing agency FAQ.

    What is a performance marketing agency?+

    A performance marketing agency is a specialist firm that drives measurable commercial outcomes (revenue, leads, customer acquisition) rather than just managing ad spend or optimising individual channels. Unlike traditional agencies, performance marketing agencies are accountable to business results, not activity metrics.

    How is Algorithm different from other performance marketing agencies in South Africa?+

    Algorithm doesn't sell channels in isolation. We redesign and run your full demand-to-revenue engine: SEO, paid media, CRO, data and AI working as one integrated system. We're a Google Premier Partner (top 3% globally), we deploy 20+ proprietary AI agents, and our team averages 12+ years of performance marketing experience.

    What services does a performance marketing agency provide?+

    Typically SEO, paid media (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, programmatic), conversion rate optimisation, data and analytics, content strategy, and increasingly AI and automation. At Algorithm, these aren't separate services. They're capabilities inside a single integrated system called the Algorithm Performance Engine.

    How do you measure performance marketing success?+

    We measure revenue impact, pipeline growth, customer acquisition cost and return on ad spend, not impressions or clicks. Every dashboard we build ties marketing activity to commercial outcomes. If it doesn't move revenue, it doesn't matter.

    Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?+

    Yes. Algorithm is headquartered in South Africa but works with clients across multiple markets. Our AI-powered Lighthouse platform monitors competitive visibility in any language and any geography.

    How does the Organic (SEO and GEO) part of the engine actually roll out?+

    The Organic component runs on a four-sprint system: Discovery in Week 0, Strategy and Technical Sprints in parallel through Month 1, Entity Sprints as the 4-week repeating engine from Month 2 onward, and Amplify Sprint continuously through Growth. The full month-by-month rollout is in our guide on how Algorithm rolls out Organic.
