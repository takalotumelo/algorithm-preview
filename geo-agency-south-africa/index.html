    The context shift

    Search just got rebuilt. GEO is how you win it.

    Generative Engine Optimisation is the practice of making your brand visible inside AI-generated answers. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google's AI Overviews don't rank pages the way Google's blue links do. They reference entity data, structured signals, citations and authoritative content to decide which brands to mention by name.

    For South African businesses, this category is being created in real time. The brands that build entity authority now, not just page authority, will be the ones AI models cite for the next decade.

    Algorithm built GEO into our core practice in 2024, before most South African agencies recognised it as a category. Traditional SEO is included, because Google's blue links still drive demand, but GEO is the lead.

    Inside the GEO practice

    What it actually takes to be cited by AI.

    AI models don't rank websites. They recommend brands. When a South African buyer asks ChatGPT for the best agency in a category, or asks Gemini to compare providers, the model draws on entity data, citation authority and structured signals to decide which brands to mention by name.

    We measure this rather than guess at it. Across 170,720 citations in South African AI answers, 87.8% of cited domains appear on only one engine, so a brand visible in ChatGPT is usually invisible in Perplexity and Claude. The dataset and method are in our South African AI citation report.

    • Entity signal building. Structured data, knowledge graph entries and authoritative citations that teach AI models who you are.
    • AI citation tracking. Proprietary monitoring of where AI models cite your brand, your competitors and category leaders, month over month.
    • Content structured for AI consumption. Q&A formatting, schema, semantic structure and entity disambiguation built in by default.

    Frequently asked questions

    Choosing a GEO agency in South Africa.

    What is a GEO agency?+

    A GEO agency optimises your brand for visibility inside AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews, building the entity authority, structured signals and citation patterns that get your brand recommended by AI models.

    How is GEO different from SEO?+

    SEO gets your website ranking in Google's blue links. GEO gets your brand cited and recommended by AI models. They share infrastructure (structured data, authoritative content, entity signals) but optimise for different surfaces. AI models don't rank pages; they cite brands.

    What is the difference between AEO and GEO?+

    AEO is about being the answer a machine extracts and reads back: featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice assistants and the direct answer in an AI Overview. GEO is about being the brand a model names when it composes a response from scratch. AEO is won on the page, GEO off it. Algorithm scopes both from the same audit.

    Do you offer AEO as well as GEO?+

    Yes, scoped together as standard rather than sold as an add-on, and reported against the same citation share metric so you can see which surface is moving.

    Why does GEO matter for South African businesses now?+

    Google AI Overviews appear above traditional results for an expanding share of commercial queries, and ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity are routinely used to shortlist brands before a buyer clicks a link. Brands that build entity authority now will be the ones AI models cite for years.

    What does a GEO engagement with Algorithm include?+

    It starts with an LLM visibility audit: how often your brand is cited across major AI models, against which queries, versus which competitors. Then we build the entity infrastructure, and track citation share monthly as your category evolves.

    How do you decide which GEO work to do first?+

    We start with the market, not the channel. Pathfinder separates information gaps from authority gaps, so the first GEO work goes where it will move citations, not where it is easiest to sell.

    How long does it take to see GEO results?+

    Entity-level changes can shift AI mention patterns within 4 to 8 weeks. Content authority signals take 3 to 6 months to compound. We report on citation share from week 4.

    Do you offer GEO without traditional SEO?+

    We offer it, but rarely recommend it, because the infrastructure that wins GEO is largely the same infrastructure that wins organic search. For businesses with strong SEO already in place, GEO-only engagements are on the table.

    Do you also offer SEO?+

    Yes. Algorithm runs SEO and GEO as one connected practice. See our SEO agency South Africa page.

    What does the GEO rollout look like after we sign?+

    GEO runs on the same four-sprint system as SEO: Discovery in Week 0, a Strategy Sprint (with a full Lighthouse GEO analysis) and Technical Sprint through Month 1, Entity Sprints from Month 2, and Amplify Sprint continuously. See how Algorithm rolls out Organic.
