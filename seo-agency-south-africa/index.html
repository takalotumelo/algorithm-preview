    Why Algorithm

    Most South African SEO agencies are still optimising for a search landscape that no longer exists.

    The way South Africans search has changed. Google still drives the majority of commercial intent, but a fast-growing share of buyers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's own AI Overviews to compare options before they ever click a link.

    The result is an SEO partnership that pays back in commercial outcomes, not vanity rankings. Reduced cost of acquisition. Demand captured at every stage of the buyer journey. Rankings that hold their ground because the foundations underneath them are sound.

    Choosing an SEO agency in South Africa.

    The questions South African business leaders ask most often when scoping an SEO partner.

    Who is the best SEO agency in South Africa?+

    The best SEO agency for any business is the one whose model matches the business's growth stage, market and ambition. Algorithm is built for South African businesses that want SEO connected to commercial outcomes, not vanity rankings, and foundations sound enough to hold as search changes.     How much does an SEO agency cost in South Africa?+

    Entry-level local SEO starts around R8,000 per month. Full-service engagements covering content, technical work and link building typically run R15,000 to R45,000+ per month. Algorithm scopes engagements to outcomes rather than fixed packages. See our SEO pricing guide for South Africa for retainer bands and the hidden costs to watch for.

    Do you work with businesses in Cape Town and Johannesburg?+

    Yes. Algorithm works with businesses across South Africa (Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Durban and regional centres) as well as South African brands targeting international markets. Our reporting, delivery rhythm and senior specialist access are the same wherever you're based.

    How long does SEO take to deliver results?+

    Most South African businesses see early ranking and impression movement within 6 to 12 weeks, meaningful traffic gains within 3 to 6 months, and compounding commercial impact from month 6 onwards. We report on leading indicators from week 4. Our SEO timeline guide covers what to expect at each stage.

    What is the difference between SEO and GEO?+

    SEO gets your website ranking in Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) gets your brand cited and recommended inside AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews. They share infrastructure but optimise for different surfaces. Our GEO and AEO work is set out on the GEO agency South Africa page.
