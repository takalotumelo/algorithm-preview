    Algorithm launches Lighthouse GEO - Africa's first AI visibility platform.

    South African performance marketing agency Algorithm has launched Lighthouse GEO, the first dedicated AI visibility platform built for African brands. Covered by BizCommunity on 23 April 2026.

    The launch

    A platform for an era when AI decides who gets recommended.

    Until now, African brands have had no reliable way to measure how they appear inside the AI assistants their customers are increasingly using to shortlist products, agencies and services. Lighthouse GEO closes that gap.

    The platform monitors brand visibility across the major AI surfaces - ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude - producing an AI Visibility Score, competitor citation share, consumer intent mapping and source authority analysis. It is built for South African and African market conditions, with proprietary research methodology developed in-house by Algorithm.

    The launch reflects a broader strategic shift at Algorithm. The agency, which has operated as a specialist performance marketing partner since 2010, has spent the last two years engineering Generative Engine Optimisation into the core of its SEO practice - long before most South African agencies recognised AI search as a category in its own right.

    The thesis is simple:Google's blue links still drive demand, but a fast-growing share of commercial discovery is now happening inside AI-generated answers. Brands that build entity authority for that layer right now will be the ones AI models cite for the next decade. Brands that wait will find the category already owned by competitors.

    Lighthouse GEO is the instrument that makes that work measurable.

    What Lighthouse GEO does

    Six capabilities. One picture of your AI visibility.

    01

    AI Visibility Score

    A 0–100 score showing how often and how favourably your brand appears across leading AI models - ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude.

    02

    Competitor Intelligence

    Track your AI citation share against direct competitors, month over month, so the gap is measurable and closable.

    03

    Consumer Intent Mapping

    Surfaces the prompts and queries buyers in your category are actually putting to AI assistants - and which brands get cited in the answers.

    04

    Source Authority Analysis

    Reveals which third-party sources AI models lean on most heavily for your category, so you know where citation acquisition matters most.

    05

    Strategic Recommendations

    Actionable next steps tied directly to where your AI visibility is leaking - not generic best practices.

    06

    Trend Monitoring

    Weekly and monthly refresh cycles so movement, gains and slippage are visible in near real time.
